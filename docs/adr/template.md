## Context

Describe the concrete problem, constraints, and unresolved product policies.

## Decision

State the selected or proposed approach and its scope. Distinguish established constraints from new proposals.

## Alternatives

Describe the credible alternatives and the reason for choosing between them.

## Consequences

Describe benefits, costs, operational responsibilities, security implications, and limits.

## Verification

State the evidence needed to validate this decision, including migration or rollback where applicable.

## Decision history

Record status changes, dates, their authority, and any superseding ADR.
